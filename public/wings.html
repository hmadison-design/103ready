Claim WINGS credit

Finished a free scenario and passed the knowledge check? Enter the completion code from the end of the scenario and the email address on your FAASafety.gov account. Credit is validated by hand by 103 Ready, an FAA Safety Team Training Provider, usually within a few days. You will see it on your My WINGS page; there is no confirmation email.

This is the only place on the site that stores an email address. It is kept until the credit is validated and used for nothing else. What the site records.
